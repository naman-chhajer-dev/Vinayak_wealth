What is it

What isRetirement Planning?

Retirement Planning through mutual funds involves systematically building a large corpus over your working years through SIP, and then systematically withdrawing from it post-retirement through a Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP). At Vinayak Wealth, we map your current income, expected retirement age, desired monthly income in retirement, and life expectancy to build a personalised retirement strategy — so you never outlive your money.

How It Works

01
🗓️

Retirement Goal Setting

We help you define your retirement age, desired monthly income, and life expectancy to set a clear corpus target.

02
📊

Corpus Calculation

We calculate the exact retirement corpus needed and the monthly SIP required to achieve it by your target date.

03
📂

Fund Selection

We build a long-term equity-heavy portfolio for the accumulation phase and shift to balanced/debt funds near retirement.

04
💸

SWP Setup

Post-retirement, we set up a Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) to generate a regular tax-efficient monthly income.

Why Choose This

Key Benefits

💸

Regular Monthly Income

SWP provides a steady monthly income in retirement — just like a pension but with market-linked returns.

📈

Inflation-Beating Returns

Equity mutual funds have historically delivered 12-15% CAGR — well above inflation — growing your corpus over time.

🧮

Tax-Efficient Withdrawals

Long-term capital gains on equity funds are taxed at only 10% — far more efficient than FD or pension income.

🔄

Flexible Retirement Age

Unlike NPS or PPF, mutual funds have no mandatory retirement age — you retire when you want.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Financial Security

A well-planned retirement corpus ensures your family's financial security even in your absence.

🌐

No Lock-In

Unlike NPS which locks funds till age 60, mutual fund investments remain accessible at any time.
